🌅 End of Day Ritual

  1. Look at your paper
  2. Mark done items ☑
  3. Move unfinished → tomorrow
  4. Snap a photo (archive)
  5. Fresh paper tomorrow

💡 Why It Works

  • Brain dump → mental load drops
  • Physical = zero distraction
  • 4 quadrants = one glance overview
  • Handwriting = stronger memory
  • Always on your desk, no app needed

📖 How to Start

  1. Grab an A4 paper. Fold it in half twice. Open it. Four quadrants.
  2. Weekly paper (Monday): Write your rules, what you're waiting on, 3 priorities, and parked ideas.
  3. Daily paper (every morning): Write today's tasks, notes, people to contact, and leave space for what you learn.
  4. Work. Glance at your paper. Check things off. Scribble notes.
  5. End of day (2 min): Mark done ☑, move unfinished →, snap a photo, trash the paper.
  6. Repeat. That's it. No app. No subscription. No sync issues.
